1. Platform Executive Summary

CouncilTrack AI is an automated student governance, daily goal tracking, evening reflection, and public complaint management platform built specifically for NavGurukul Dantewada. It enforces accountability across campus coordinator bodies, provides real-time transparency to campus leadership, and provides a frictionless channel for student voice.

🛡️ Role-Based Access Control
Strict separation between Campus Administrators (ADMIN), Council Coordinators (COUNCIL), and Students (Public Access with zero login friction).
🎯 Daily Goals & Evening Reflections
Morning goal creation paired with mandatory evening reflection checklists. Goals marked incomplete strictly require explicit reasons.
🔒 Confidential Feedback System
Public feedback form for all 14 councils with anonymous options. Administrative notes are cryptographically shielded from council members.
📊 Automated Progress Formulas
Monday-to-Sunday weekly and monthly analytics computed dynamically using the PRD standard formula: (Completed / (Completed + Incomplete)) * 100.

3. Core Portals & Daily Operating Workflows

3.1 Public Student Voice & Complaint Portal (/feedback)

Students access a single, common feedback link without needing accounts or logging in. They can select any of the 14 councils, choose between General Feedback, Suggestion, or Complaint, and optionally submit anonymously. Once submitted, feedback is immediately dispatched via WebSockets to the Admin dashboard and council views.

3.2 Council Member Daily Cycle (/council/*)

🌅 Morning: Daily Goal Setting (/council/goals)
Coordinators establish actionable goals for their council before morning assembly. Each goal includes a title, description, priority badge (HIGH, MEDIUM, LOW), and targeted outcome.
🌆 Evening: Daily Reflection (/council/reflection)
Mandatory check-in before curfew. The coordinator reviews today's goals. Marking a goal COMPLETED updates audit history; marking INCOMPLETE enforces entering a mandatory impediment reason.
📈 Progress Tracking (/council/weekly-progress)
Automatic weekly consistency percentage calculated across active weekdays (Monday to Sunday) with day-by-day historical records.
💬 Council Complaints View (/council/feedback)
Coordinators can inspect and act upon student feedback submitted specifically for their council. Private administrator notes remain strictly hidden.

3.3 Campus Administrator Control Center (/admin/*)

📊 Executive Dashboard (/admin/dashboard)
Real-time metrics: Total Councils (14), Active Coordinators (25), Today's Active Goals, and Overall Campus Completion Rate.
👥 Member Management (/admin/members)
Create new coordinators, re-assign coordinators across councils, edit designations, and toggle ACTIVE / INACTIVE account status.
🏛️ Council Management (/admin/councils)
Create and edit council domains, adjust descriptions, and view current member rosters per council.
🎯 Council Goals & Audit Timeline (/admin/goals)
Inspect goal cards across all councils. Click any goal to open the audit trail showing who changed the status, old vs new state, and timestamps.
Admin Confidential Notes Feature: Under /admin/feedback, administrators can update complaint statuses (NEW ➔ UNDER_REVIEW ➔ RESOLVED) and attach private notes (e.g. staff meetings, student counseling notes). These notes are cryptographically omitted from council member payloads.

4. Discord Webhook Integration

CouncilTrack AI includes native Discord webhook support to push real-time embed cards for all campus milestones.

Step-by-step Setup:

  1. Open your Discord Server ➔ Channel Settings (Gear Icon) ➔ Integrations ➔ Webhooks.
  2. Click New Webhook, assign a name (e.g. CouncilTrack Bot), and copy the Webhook URL.
  3. Paste the URL into counciltrack-ai/backend/.env:
# Single Webhook for all alerts: DISCORD_WEBHOOK_URL=https://discord.com/api/webhooks/1234567890/abcdefghijklmnopqrstuvwxyz # Or Dedicated Channels: DISCORD_WEBHOOK_GOALS=https://discord.com/api/webhooks/... # Target: #council-goals DISCORD_WEBHOOK_COMPLETIONS=https://discord.com/api/webhooks/...# Target: #goal-completions DISCORD_WEBHOOK_REFLECTIONS=https://discord.com/api/webhooks/...# Target: #weekly-reflections

Supported Automated Discord Embeds:

🎯 Goal Created / Completed
Sent when a coordinator sets a morning priority or marks it completed during evening reflection, tagging Council name and priority.
📝 Evening Reflection Submitted
Pushed when an evening review is logged, showcasing achievements, key learnings, and improvement plans.
