Council Coordinators Operations Manual • Daily Operating Guidelines

1. Welcome & Operating Principle

CouncilTrack AI enables campus coordinators to log daily commitments, conduct evening reviews, and resolve student feedback. Every coordinator is accountable for their domain's daily momentum.

🌅 Morning: Set Daily Goals (07:00 AM)
Navigate to /council/goals. Set 1–3 concrete, actionable goals for your council before assembly. Assign priorities (HIGH, MEDIUM, LOW) and define expected outcomes.
🌆 Evening: Daily Reflection (08:30 PM)
Navigate to /council/reflection. Mark goals as COMPLETED ✓ or INCOMPLETE ✕. Incomplete goals require an explicit impediment reason. Log your key achievements and learnings.
📊 Track Weekly Progress
Inspect /council/weekly-progress. Your weekly score is computed dynamically across Monday to Sunday: (Completed / (Completed + Incomplete)) * 100.
💬 Review Student Feedback
Inspect /council/feedback to see complaints and suggestions submitted by campus students specifically for your council domain.
