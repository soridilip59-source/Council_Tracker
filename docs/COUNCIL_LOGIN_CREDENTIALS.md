## Quick Login & Password Reset Instructions

1. **Login:** Open `/council/login`, enter your **Email** (or **User ID** / **Handle**) and **Current Password** (`Admin@123`), then click **Login to Council Portal**.
2. **Reset / Change Password (Council):** After logging in, go to **Profile & Password** (`/council/settings`) in the sidebar, enter your **Current Password** and your **New Password** (minimum 4 characters), and click **Change Password**.
3. **Admin Password Reset:** The Admin can view each council's current password and reset it anytime from **Council Management** (`/admin/councils`) in the Admin Portal.
